Hi,

I'm a developer and designer living in Oklahoma City. In my work I operate on a principle that functionally useful things should look as good as they work, and that good looking things should be entirely functional.

Interfaces, Interfaces, & Interfaces

I'm not sure I'm really tied to a specific framework or stack currently, honing the skill of picking the appropriate stack for the job is most important rather than being locked into a specific framework.

Fluent in Animation

Physical Copies also Available
